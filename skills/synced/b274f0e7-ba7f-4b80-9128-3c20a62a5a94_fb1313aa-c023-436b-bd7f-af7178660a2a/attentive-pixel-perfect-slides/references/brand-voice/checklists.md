# Checklists

Use the brand voice checklist while writing and the final QA checklist before
publishing or sharing externally.

## Brand voice checklist

**Clarity and purpose**

- Would someone outside our team immediately understand this?
- Does every sentence move the reader forward?
- Is there fluff to cut?
- Have simpler words replaced technical or industry-heavy language?
- Is the intended action or takeaway clear?

**Brand voice and personality**

- Does this sound like a real person, not AI or a corporate robot?
- Does it show expertise without showing off?
- Is it warm and professional?
- Does it feel like Attentive understands the reader's challenge?

**Customer-centered thinking**

- Does the copy focus on what the reader cares about?
- Does it connect technology to better customer experiences?
- Does it speak to outcomes, not just features?
- Does it remember there's a real person behind every data point?

**Strategic alignment**

- Does this align with "Marketing made personal"?
- Does it balance innovation with practicality?
- Does it reinforce that technology amplifies human understanding?
- Does it build trust through both message and tone?

**Quality standards**

- Has the copy been read aloud?
- Are there any words to cut?
- Are grammar, typos, and consistency checked?
- Would the writer feel proud to put their name on it?

**Final test**

- If a busy marketer received this, would it respect their time?
- Does this feel helpful rather than salesy or interruptive?
- Is it easy for the reader to understand, say yes, or take action?

## Final QA checklist before publishing

Review for:

- Correct brand name: Attentive
- Correct legal name usage only when needed
- Correct trademarks on first mention
- Approved product names
- No banned or discouraged terms
- No unsupported claims; no invented stats
- No jargon or corporate filler
- Customer value is clear; outcome is clear
- Tone fits the channel
- Sentences are concise
- Oxford comma is used
- Em dashes have no spaces
- Ampersands are used only when appropriate
- Time, dates, numbers, capitalization, and hyphenation follow the guide
- CTA is clear
- Copy sounds natural when read aloud

## Before/after reference

- **Generic → Attentive.** Before: "Leverage our AI-powered platform to optimize
  omnichannel engagement across the customer lifecycle." After: "Use Attentive AI™ to
  reach customers with more relevant messages across SMS, email, RCS, and push."
- **Feature-first → outcome-first.** Before: "Our platform includes advanced identity
  resolution and segmentation capabilities." After: "Recognize more shoppers, understand
  what they care about, and send messages that feel relevant in the moment."
- **Too cold → human.** Before: "Attentive's AI-driven platform delivers enterprise-grade
  solutions." After: "Attentive helps marketers turn customer signals into messages that
  feel personal—and drive performance."
- **Too hypey → confident.** Before: "Supercharge your marketing with revolutionary AI
  that transforms everything." After: "Let AI agents handle the busywork of
  personalization so your team can focus on strategy."
- **Too negative → supportive.** Before: "Brands that fail to personalize will lose
  customer loyalty." After: "Brands that understand what each customer needs are better
  positioned to build lasting loyalty."

## Remember

- The customer is the hero of our story, not Attentive.
- Technology amplifies human understanding. It doesn't replace it.
- We're peer-mentors, not professors or salespeople.
- Every word should earn its place. Brevity is powerful.
- Marketing is about people as much as metrics.
- When in doubt, read it aloud. If it doesn't sound like something you'd say to help a
  colleague solve a real problem, revise it.
