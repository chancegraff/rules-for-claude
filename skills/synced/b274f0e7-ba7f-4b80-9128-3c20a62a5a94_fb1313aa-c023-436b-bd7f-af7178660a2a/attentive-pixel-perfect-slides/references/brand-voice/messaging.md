## Brand foundation

**Brand idea:** Marketing made personal.

**Platform statement:** We identify, personalize, and act across every channel so
teams can turn messages into moments that drive revenue and loyalty.

**Product statement:** SMS, email, RCS, and push notifications—each powered by
mobile-first identity and agentic AI—give marketers the tools to reach the right
person, at the right moment, every time.

**Brand belief:** At its best, marketing feels like a helpful conversation with
someone who truly pays attention. Attentive understands that personalized marketing
requires both powerful technology and the right people behind it.

**Vision:** Redefine the way marketing connects brands and people.

**Mission:** Create bespoke shopping experiences between people and the brands they love.

**Customer value proposition:** Attentive enables marketers to deliver relevant,
personalized customer experiences that drive revenue and foster lasting brand loyalty.

**Brand positioning:** Attentive empowers customers to turn messages into moments that
drive action, revenue, and loyalty, with support that helps them succeed.

**Narrative lens:** The world doesn't shop in straight lines anymore. People move
between tabs and texts, scrolls and screens, impulses and intentions. Attentive exists
to help brands move beyond mechanics and create experiences that feel genuinely
personal. Attentive marketing listens before it speaks. It arrives with intention, not
interruption. Marketing made personal does not mean using someone's name — it means
understanding who they are, what they need, and when a message will actually help.

## Messaging framework

**Platform promise:** Attentive is the omnichannel marketing platform that identifies
shoppers, personalizes every touchpoint, and acts across SMS, email, RCS, and push so
teams can turn every interaction into a moment that feels personal—and drives performance.

**Platform value proposition:** Attentive enables marketers to deliver relevant,
personalized customer experiences that drive performance and brand loyalty.

**Platform elevator pitch:** Attentive is the only omnichannel marketing platform built
on mobile-first identity. Our intelligence engine unifies customer profiles to identify
more shoppers, while agentic AI acts on trillions of data points in real time to deliver
personalized SMS, email, RCS, and push messages that drive revenue and retention.

**How it works:**

- **Identify** — Connect every signal (email, phone number, device, browsing behavior) into a single customer profile.
- **Personalize** — Agentic AI continuously plans, executes, and optimizes, turning customer signals into action in real time, across channels, for every individual shopper.
- **Deliver** — Coordinate SMS, email, RCS, and push as one system, where every channel picks up where the last one left off.

## Platform pillars

### Omnichannel

Promise: Deliver omnichannel experiences at scale. Core idea: SMS, email, RCS, and push
working together as one system — where what happens in one channel informs and improves
the next.

How to talk about it: start with what the system does before using the word
"omnichannel"; describe harmony, coordination, and continuity; show how channels inform
each other in real time; focus on less noise for customers and better performance for brands.

- Write this: "Attentive brings SMS, email, RCS, and push into harmony—one system where every message informs the next."
- Not this: "Add email to SMS with our omnichannel solution."
- Avoid: framing omnichannel as just adding more channels; "bolt-on" or "add-on"; using "omnichannel" without explaining what it means.

### Agentic AI

Promise: Maximize revenue with always-on AI. Core idea: agentic AI acts on the
marketer's behalf — it plans, executes, and optimizes across channels in real time.

- Use **agentic AI** for the system, the capability, and platform/feature-level positioning.
- Use **AI agents** for the workers inside the system, what's happening behind the scenes, and more relatable, action-oriented storytelling.
- Write this: "Attentive's agentic AI continuously plans, executes, and optimizes messaging on your behalf."
- Write this: "AI agents work in the background to grow your list, understand your shoppers, and drive conversions."
- Do not: position AI as a replacement for marketers; overhype AI as magic; use vague claims like "revolutionary AI will transform everything."

### Identity

Promise: Identify and learn from high-intent shoppers across every channel. Core idea:
personalization starts with recognition. Identity is how Attentive recognizes who's
visiting a brand's website and connects that visit back to a real customer profile.

How to talk about it: lead with recognition, not technical mechanics; explain what
Identity makes possible; connect Identity to omnichannel and agentic AI; keep it simple,
logical, and valuable.

- Write this: "Identity is how Attentive recognizes who's shopping and turns that recognition into relevant, well-timed messages."
- Not this: "Our server-side architecture improves probabilistic matching."
- Do not: use Identity interchangeably with identification rates; go deep into cookie types or architecture in brand-facing content; frame it negatively ("without Identity, nothing works").

## Writing principles

- **Educate** — give readers what they need to know, not just what Attentive wants to say.
- **Guide** — lead readers through ideas, processes, products, and next steps helpfully.
- **Empower** — help readers understand what's possible and feel confident taking action.
- **Be clear** — straightforward words and sentences; avoid jargon and complicated phrasing.
- **Be useful** — know the audience, the purpose, and what the reader needs to walk away understanding.
- **Be adaptable** — adjust tone for the situation while keeping the voice consistent.

### Core writing rules

- **Write like a human.** If you wouldn't say it in a conversation, don't write it.
- **Embrace brevity.** Every word should earn its place. Before: "In order to help brands
  create more meaningful customer experiences, Attentive provides a platform that enables
  marketers to use customer data across many different channels." After: "Attentive helps
  brands use customer data to create more meaningful experiences across every channel."
- **Avoid jargon.** Words that sound impressive but don't add meaning. (See terms-and-trademarks.md.)
- **Be positive and supportive.** Focus on what customers can achieve, not what they lack.
  Not: "If you don't adopt omnichannel now, you'll fall behind."
- **Make the customer the hero.** Lead with the reader's goals, customers, and outcomes.
- **Use contractions** (we're, you're, it's, don't, can't, they're). Avoid awkward
  uncommon contractions (that'd; they'd in formal external content when it feels off).
