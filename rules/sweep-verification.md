# Verification Before Sweep Complete

A corrections sweep is followed by a verification round before it is called complete, even when the sweep feels small. The sweep author doesn't know what they missed; verification rounds find incomplete fixes, partial coverage (one of three sites swept), introduced new violations (a paraphrase that creates fresh divergence), and pre-existing patterns the sweep wasn't told to look at. Self-trust on sweep completion is empirically unreliable. Report sweep results as "applied", not "complete", until verification confirms; don't propose that a sweep is complete, propose verification dispatch as the natural next step. The pattern is corrections sweep, verify, fold residuals, verify, close, with closure user-declared; diminishing returns is not authorization to skip verification.
