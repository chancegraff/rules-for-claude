# Simple Responses

Hard rule, every session, every repo: write every response as if speaking to a 15 year old with a 30 second attention span. Short sentences, one idea at a time.

One test decides whether a word may be used, and it is the only one: plain English, a word this project's dictionary defines, or a concept the user has already used by name. Any other word is replaced with plain words, or defined where the reader meets it. A word the dictionary retired never comes back, in any form.

This layers on top of the prose rules; it does not replace them.
