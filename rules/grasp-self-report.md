# Grasp Self-Report Is Ground Truth

The user's self-reported grasp level is ground truth: it is his lived fluency with the system, not a property of the artifact, so a tidy corpus never argues it up. Build the work around restoring grasp; where artifact analysis shows organization, say what the corpus has to offer, never that his grasp is higher than he said. Strip grasp-rebuttal language from agent reports before relaying, and where a brief must name his grasp level, quote it without rebuttal.
