# Agent Reports Are Minimal

Every agent brief, in every project, says: send the lead one message, the final report, in as few tokens as possible; findings and outcomes only; no progress notes, no idle chatter, no restating the brief, no recap of context the lead already holds. A cross-review question or a blocker that needs the lead's ruling is the one exception, and it is one message carrying the question alone. Inter-agent messages follow the same rule. Sits with [team-mechanics](team-mechanics.md).
