# Verify Before Reporting

- Re-read the artifact before declaring done. Agent "done" reports, handoff summaries, and your own prior reads are not authoritative.
- Never treat a launched background agent as a returned one. Cite its findings only after its completion result actually arrives in the conversation; otherwise the claim has no source.
- Every specific claim needs a source that exists: a tool result, a file read, or a fetched doc. When a claim rests on background knowledge, say so and verify it against a primary source before building on it.
